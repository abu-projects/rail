## Sam

Cycling is my longest-standing love affair. This passion, combined with my technical education and a keen interest in metalworking, provided the perfect breeding ground a few years ago to embark on the adventure of building my own first frame. Surrounded by the right friends – first and foremost Paddy, Steff, Flori and Blizzy – I enjoy the craftsmanship that provides a balance to my day job and the privilege of riding my own creations on tours and in races. The workshop, with its hand files, hole saws, jigs and the high concentration of argon in the air, makes me lose track of time and allows me to pay close attention to the small details.
