# Privacy.

Draft · Hosting and data-processing details must be completed before publication.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact form. RAIL Cycles is based in Central Switzerland.

## Email enquiries

Submitting the form sends your name, email address, selected model and message by email through Cloudflare and Resend so your enquiry can be handled. Please do not send sensitive information. The website does not maintain its own message database. Resend retains message content and delivery logs for 30 days; a copy remains in the recipient’s mailbox.

## Cookies and external content

This website version uses no analytics or advertising scripts and does not itself set cookies. Language selection uses page links. Images and fonts are served with the website; maps and social-media content are not embedded.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes technical connection data, such as IP addresses and access times, across its global network to serve and protect the website. Email delivery uses Resend with the Ireland sending region.

## Your requests

For access, correction or deletion of your personal data, please get in touch using the contact form.
