# The RAIL family.

From gravel to enduro. Four models with local roots.

Made by hand.

The Rail family is TIG-welded by hand from 25CrMo4 Reynolds and Columbus tubing. The tubes are carefully selected for the locations and loads of each model. Machined parts are made in a town near lake Zurich. Dropouts follow the UDH standard. Every frame is cathodic dip-coated and then powder-coated for ultimate protection against corrosion and wear. The bottom bracket is engraved with serial number and your name. Give me your favourite RAL code of your choice for the powder coating.

ROM prices are for the finished, coated and machined frame.

02 / Trail / Enduro

## Lopper

The chosen design concept of the supported single-pivot suspension impresses with its simplicity and makes optimum use of the 4130’s material properties.

Rear travel: 155 to 160 mm (depending on shock travel and chainstay length).

Large-diameter deep-groove ball bearings ensure good responsiveness of the rear triangle and a long service life. The bearings are easily accessible from the outside, allowing for optimal maintainability. The main pivot bearings are integrated into the chainstays to maximise the distance between the bearing points, thereby optimising the lever ratio under lateral loads (Scandi Flick).

The geometry is tailored to the rider in such a way as to result in a balanced centre of gravity in relation to both the riding geometry and the suspension kinematics.

The lengths of the main frame and the chainstays are tuned to the customer to achieve a perfect balance for both uphill and downhill riding.

A flat head angle better absorbs impacts at the front, and the steering geometry is maintained for longer when braking.

Front suspension travel: forks with 160 to 170 mm

An enduro trail bike that’s also happy to compete in the occasional enduro race.

ROM price: CHF 3’100

ROM prices are for the finished, coated and machined frame.

03 / Down Country / Trail

## Muetterschwandeberg

Based on the same full-suspension concept as the Lopper, the Muetterschwandeberg (MsB) also embodies Elegant Symplicity. This makes ideal use of the material properties of 4130 steel.

Thanks to the shorter travel and a rear triangle pivot point deliberately set slightly higher, the MsB rides with a stiffer and more responsive feel; it climbs technical trails with the shock open, whilst surprising with plenty of traction.

Rear travel: 125 to 130 mm (depending on shock travel and chainstay length).

The geometry is tailored to the rider in such a way as to create a balanced centre of gravity in relation to both the riding geometry and the suspension kinematics.

Longer main frame and longer chainstays, particularly for taller riders, result in a perfect balance between uphill and downhill performance.

A flat head angle absorbs impacts at the front more effectively, and the steering geometry is maintained for longer when braking.

Front suspension travel: forks with 140 to 150 mm

A trail and down-country bike with an incredibly wide range of use. From the winding trails of the Jura, through the paths of Central Switzerland, to the trails of Valais, the MsB is the ultimate ride!

ROM price: CHF 3’100

ROM prices are for the finished, coated and machined frame, excluding rear shock.
